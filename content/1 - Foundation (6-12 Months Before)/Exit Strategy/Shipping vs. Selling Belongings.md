# Topic Title

## 1. Overview

- **Timeline:** (e.g., 2 months before departure)
- **Goal:** (1-sentence summary of the objective)

## 2. Importance 

- **The Benefit:** (What do you gain by doing this right?)
    1. Avoiding the expense and hassle of transporting items that are not essential for your stay abroad.
    2. Selling belongings can provide additional funds for your relocation expenses or costs during your stay abroad.
- **The Risk:** (What is the penalty/consequence of failing or delaying? e.g., Fines, deportation, homelessness.)
    1. Holding on to unnecessary items could result in storage fees or make moving more difficult and expensive.
    2. Selling belongings too late may not yield a satisfactory price or force you to sell essential items.

## 3. Prerequisites 
*What you need to have ready before starting.*

- **Documents:**
    - None
- **Costs:** 
	- Estimated fees for shipping, selling, and storage
- **Dependencies:**
    - None

## 4. Instructions 
*The step-by-step execution plan.*

1. **Step One:** Make an inventory of belongings you are considering shipping or selling.
2. **Step Two:** Research shipping companies to estimate the cost and time for transporting items to your destination.
3. **Step Three:** Determine the estimated value of each item by researching local second-hand market prices.
4. **Step Four:** Weigh the costs, benefits, and inconvenience of shipping versus selling each item.
5. **Step Five:** Choose a suitable method for disposal or transport based on your decision in Step 4.
    1. (Optional) If you choose to sell items, consider using online marketplaces, local classifieds, or hosting a garage sale.
   
> [!TIP] Pro Tip
> Prioritize selling high-value items that may be expensive to ship, while considering shipping smaller, sentimental items.

## 5. Pitfalls
*Common mistakes and "anti-patterns" to avoid.*

> [!DANGER] Things to Avoid
>- **Mistake 1:** Failing to research shipping or selling costs could result in unexpected expenses.
>- **Mistake 2:** Selling items too late may not yield a satisfactory price, forcing you to hold on to unnecessary belongings.
