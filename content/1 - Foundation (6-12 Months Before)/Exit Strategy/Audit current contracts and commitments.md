# Topic Title

## 1. Overview

- **Timeline:** 6-9 months before departure.
- **Goal:** Review your existing contracts and commitments in all areas of life and identify any that could pose as potential liabilities during your stay abroad. (Gym memberships, phone plans, internet)

## 2. Importance 

- **The Benefit:** (What do you gain by doing this right?)
    1. A clear understanding of your current financial obligations will help you plan for any necessary payments or cancelations before departing.
    2. Avoid potential issues with unpaid bills, late fees, or contract violations that could harm your credit score or cause difficulties during your stay abroad.
- **The Risk:** (What is the penalty/consequence of failing or delaying? e.g., Fines, deportation, homelessness.)
    1. Neglecting to audit your contracts and commitments may result in unpaid bills, late fees, or contract violations that could impact your credit score and financial stability.
    2. Failing to cancel unnecessary services before departure could lead to ongoing expenses during your stay abroad.

## 3. Prerequisites 
*What you need to have ready before starting.*

- **Documents:**
	- None
- **Costs:** 
	- None
- **Dependencies:** 
	- None

## 4. Instructions 
*The step-by-step execution plan.*

1. **Step One:** Review your current contracts and commitments by gathering bills, statements, or other relevant documents for each service provider.
2. **Step Two:** Determine if any of the contracts have termination provisions that require notice before canceling or if there are penalties associated with early cancellation.
3. **Step Three:** Identify which services will no longer be needed during your stay abroad, and create a plan to cancel those services in a timely manner.
    1. (Optional) If necessary, arrange for temporary hold or transfer of service if applicable (e.g., phone number).
   
> [!TIP] Pro Tip
> Utilize online chat support or email for service providers when possible, as it may be quicker and more convenient than calling during business hours.

## 5. Pitfalls
*Common mistakes and "anti-patterns" to avoid.*

> [!DANGER] Things to Avoid
>- **Mistake 1:** Failing to review all contracts and commitments could result in unnoticed obligations or expenses during your stay abroad.
>- **Mistake 2:** Not giving the required notice period for canceling a contract may result in penalties or ongoing expenses
