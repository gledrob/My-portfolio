# Topic Title

## 1. Overview

- **Timeline:** 6-8 months before departure
- **Goal:** Research and apply accommodation options that are available to you and satisfy your needs as you may not have this opportunity later.
## 2. Importance 

- **The Benefit:** (What do you gain by doing this right?)
    1. Ensuring a place to live upon arrival will help alleviate stress during relocation.
    2. Having sufficient time to compare different options will allow you to make an informed decision and secure the best accommodation possible.
- **The Risk:** (What is the penalty/consequence of failing or delaying? e.g., Fines, deportation, homelessness.)
    1. Limited availability of suitable accommodations may force you to accept less desirable options or pay higher prices due to delayed application.
    2. Arriving without prearranged accommodation could result in difficulties finding a place to stay and cause financial strain.

## 3. Prerequisites 
*What you need to have ready before starting.*

- **Documents:**
	- None
- **Costs:** 
	- None
- **Dependencies:** [Source of Funds and Budget](obsidian://open?file=Portfolio%20website%2FISG%20content%2FSource%20of%20Funds%20and%20Budget.md) (Having a clear understanding of your financial situation will help determine the type and cost of accommodation.)
## 4. Instructions 
*The step-by-step execution plan.*

1. **Step One:** Research various types of accommodations available in the target city or region, such as apartments, houses, dormitories, hostels, or homestays, based on your preferences and budget.
2. **Step Two:** Investigate the application processes for each accommodation option to determine any specific requirements (e.g., application deadlines, documentation, fees).
3. **Step Three:** Apply for the accommodations that meet your needs and are available within your preferred timeframe, ensuring you submit all necessary documents and fees on time.
   
> [!TIP] Pro Tip
> Reach out to fellow students or local community members for recommendations and advice on finding suitable accommodations.

## 5. Pitfalls
*Common mistakes and "anti-patterns" to avoid.*

> [!DANGER] Things to Avoid
> - **Mistake 1:** Failing to research available options may result in settling for less desirable or more expensive accommodations.
> - **Mistake 2:** Relying on outdated or insufficient information could lead to difficulties in finding suitable accommodations.
