# Topic Title

## 1. Overview

- **Timeline:** 6-8 months before departure
- **Goal:** Decide what kind of accommodation you need for your type of stay in the foreign country and whether you need a temporary accommodation upon arrival.

## 2. Importance 

- **The Benefit:** (What do you gain by doing this right?)
    1. Finding suitable accommodation in advance can help alleviate stress and provide a sense of security during relocation.
    2. Ensuring that the accommodations meet your needs and budget will contribute to a positive living experience.
- **The Risk:** (What is the penalty/consequence of failing or delaying? e.g., Fines, deportation, homelessness.)
    1. Delayed arrival due to difficulties finding suitable accommodation.
    2. Financial strain due to overpaying for accommodations or making hasty decisions based on limited information.

## 3. Prerequisites 
*What you need to have ready before starting.*

- **Documents:**
	- None
- **Costs:** 
	- None
- **Dependencies:** [Source of Funds and Budget](obsidian://open?file=Portfolio%20website%2FISG%20content%2FSource%20of%20Funds%20and%20Budget.md) (Having a clear understanding of your financial situation will help determine the type and cost of accommodation.)

## 4. Instructions 
*The step-by-step execution plan.*

1. **Step One:** Research different types of accommodations available in the target city or region, such as apartments, houses, dormitories, hostels, or homestays, based on your preferences and budget.
2. **Step Two:** Determine whether you require temporary accommodation upon arrival (e.g., a hotel or hostel) to give yourself time to find long-term accommodations.
3. **Step Three:** Compare various options in terms of price, location, amenities, and lease terms to make an informed decision on the type and length of accommodation that best suits your needs.
   
> [!TIP] Pro Tip
> Reach out to fellow students or local community members for recommendations and advice on finding suitable accommodations.
## 5. Pitfalls
*Common mistakes and "anti-patterns" to avoid.*

> [!DANGER] Things to Avoid
> - **Mistake 1:** Failing to research available options may result in settling for less desirable or more expensive accommodations.
> - **Mistake 2:** Relying on outdated or insufficient information could lead to difficulties in finding suitable accommodations.
