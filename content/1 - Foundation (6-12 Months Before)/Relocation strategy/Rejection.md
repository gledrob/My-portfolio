# What do I do if I get rejected?

## 1. Overview

- **Timeline:** 6-9 months before departure
- **Goal:** Consistently document the relocation process procedures for future reference (e.g. visa applications, forms, application requirements and your answers to them).

## 2. Importance 

- **The Benefit:** Organizes the application process, making it easier for future reference.
- **The Risk:** (What is the penalty/consequence of failing or delaying?) Failing to track applications may lead to missed deadlines, increased stress, and potential complications during the relocation process.

## 3. Prerequisites 
*What you need to have ready before starting.*

- **Documents:** None
- **Costs:** None
- **Dependencies:** (What specific task must be completed _before_ this one?) Completing [[Prepare Core Documents]] as prerequisite ensures the availability of required documents for application purposes.

## 4. Instructions 
*The step-by-step execution plan.*

1. **Step One:** Organize a system to track your applications (e.g., using an Excel spreadsheet, Google Docs, or a note-taking app like Obsidian).
2. **Step Two:** Document each application you submit, including the type of application, date submitted, required documents, deadlines, and status updates.
3. **Step Three:** Regularly review and update your tracking system as new applications are added or current ones progress through the process.
   
> [!TIP] Pro Tip
> Store your tracking system in a secure location (e.g., password-protected cloud storage) to ensure it is readily accessible when needed during the relocation process.
## 5. Pitfalls
*Common mistakes and "anti-patterns" to avoid.*

> [!DANGER] Things to Avoid
> - **Mistake 1:** Failing to update your tracking system regularly may result in missed deadlines or forgotten applications.
> - **Mistake 2:** Neglecting to include all necessary information for each application (e.g., not documenting the type of application, date submitted, and status updates) makes it difficult to refer back to them later on.
