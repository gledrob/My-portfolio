# Topic Title

## 1. Overview

- **Timeline:**  6-10 months before departure
- **Goal:** Research and prepare in advance the core documents required for the relocation process procedures (f.e. related to finances and health insurance).

## 2. Importance 

- **The Benefit:** You will have a set of documents needed for relocation beforehand.
- **The Risk:** Rejections or delays due to missing documents, incomplete applications.

## 3. Prerequisites 
*What you need to have ready before starting.*

- **Documents:**
	- Valid identification document (e.g. Passport, Residence Permit)
	- Proof of residence (if abroad)
	- Proof of health Insurance 
	- Proof of Acceptance/Admission to institution
	- Proof of Funds
	- Proof of language proficiency (e.g. language certificates)
	- Academic Records
	- Letter of Motivation/Purpose/Intent
	- Curriculum Vitae
	- Standardised Test Scores
	- Biometric photos
- **Costs:** 
	- Valid ID fees
	- Health insurance payments
	- Language certificate fees
	- Standardised Test fees
	- Photos fees
	- Additional undefined fees
- **Dependencies:** 
	- [[Specifics of your moving process]]
	- [[Prepare Proof of Funds]]

## 4. Instructions 
*The step-by-step execution plan.*

1. **Step One:** Research the core documents required for the destination country and create a checklist. You can consult resources such as embassy websites or the International Student Relocation Framework v1.1 for more information.
2. **Step Two:** Gather all necessary documents from the prerequisites section and ensure they are up to date. If any documents are missing, apply for them according to the respective application processes.
3. **Step Three:** Obtain any missing documents by following the appropriate application processes, paying associated fees, and meeting all requirements. Keep track of document submission deadlines and follow up on any outstanding applications.
   
> [!TIP] Pro Tip
> Begin this process early to avoid delays in obtaining necessary documents and potential visa denials. Additionally, be aware that some document requirements may change based on the destination country or institution, so it's essential to stay informed and adapt your strategy accordingly

## 5. Pitfalls
*Common mistakes and "anti-patterns" to avoid.*

> [!DANGER] Things to Avoid
> - **Mistake 1:** Hoping for a successful outcome with missing/delayed documents. 
> - **Mistake 1:** Failing to check the specific core document requirements of the destination country, which may lead to visa denials or delays.
> - **Mistake 2:** Relying on outdated documents, as institutions and embassies may require up-to-date versions of certain documents.
> - **Mistake 3:** Neglecting to budget for document-related fees, which can add unexpected expenses to your relocation costs.
