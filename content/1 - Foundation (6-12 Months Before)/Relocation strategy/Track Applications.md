# Topic Title

## 1. Overview

- **Timeline:** 6-9 months before departure
- **Goal:** Consistently document the relocation process procedures for future reference (e.g. visa applications, forms, application requirements and your answers to them).

## 2. Importance 

- **The Benefit:** (What do you gain by doing this right?)
    
    1. Organized and streamlined relocation process.
    2. Reduced stress and confusion during the application process.
    3. Access to valuable information for future references or helping others.
- **The Risk:** (What is the penalty/consequence of failing or delaying? e.g., Fines, deportation, homelessness.)
    
    1. Increased risk of missing crucial deadlines or requirements.
    2. Inefficient use of time and resources during the application process.
    3. Potential complications in the relocation process due to a lack of proper documentation.

## 3. Prerequisites 
*What you need to have ready before starting.*

- **Documents:**
    - None
- **Costs:**
    - None
- **Dependencies:** (What specific task must be completed _before_ this one?)
    - Establishing a clear relocation strategy [International Student Relocation Framework v1.1](obsidian://open?file=Portfolio%20website%2FISG%20content%2FInternational%20Student%20Relocation%20Framework%20v1.1.md).

## 4. Instructions 
*The step-by-step execution plan.*

1. **Step One:** Familiarize yourself with the application requirements and procedures for each country or institution you will be dealing with.
2. **Step Two:** Create a document or system to record all relevant information, including deadlines, application forms, required documents, and your responses to any questions posed on the applications.
3. **Step Three:** Regularly update this document as new information becomes available or when you complete an application.
   
> [!TIP] Pro Tip
> - Consider using a note-taking app like Obsidian to organize your relocation process.
> - Collaborate with others, such as family members or friends, who might be going through the same process.
> - Create reminders for upcoming deadlines and set aside dedicated time each week to work on applications.

## 5. Pitfalls
*Common mistakes and "anti-patterns" to avoid.*

> [!DANGER] Things to Avoid
> - **Mistake 1:** Failing to research all requirements for each institution or country you will be dealing with.
> - **Mistake 2:** Neglecting to update your documentation as new information becomes available.
> - **Mistake 3:** Relying solely on memory instead of maintaining a documented record of the application process
