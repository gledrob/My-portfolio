# Topic Title

## 1. Overview

- **Timeline:** 6 months before departure
- **Goal:** Set up a digital and physical repository of your prepared documents, that you will use and update while abroad.

## 2. Importance 

- **The Benefit:** (What do you gain by doing this right?)
    1. Having copies and backups of essential documents will help ensure that important information is always available when needed.
    2. Reducing the likelihood of losing or misplacing critical documents.
- **The Risk:** (What is the penalty/consequence of failing or delaying? e.g., Fines, deportation, homelessness.)
    1. Losing important documents can cause delays or difficulties during your stay abroad.
    2. Not having copies or backups could result in inconvenience and potential financial loss if documents are lost or stolen.

## 3. Prerequisites 
*What you need to have ready before starting.*

- **Documents:**
    - All necessary documents from [Prepare Supporting Documents](obsidian://open?file=Portfolio%20website%2FISG%20content%2FPrepare%20Supporting%20Documents.md) (Assume that all documents have already been prepared.)
- **Costs:** 
	- Print costs
	- Cloud/Digital Storage provider costs
- **Dependencies:** 
	- None

## 4. Instructions 
*The step-by-step execution plan.*

1. **Step One:** Create a digital repository of your prepared documents on a cloud storage platform such as Google Drive or Dropbox, ensuring the files are easily accessible and secure.
2. **Step Two:** Print physical copies of essential documents for safekeeping and easy access when traveling without internet connectivity.
3. **Step Three:** Maintain an updated version of your digital and physical repositories as you collect new documents or make changes to existing ones while abroad.
   
> [!TIP] Pro Tip
> Use a password manager to securely store the login credentials for your cloud storage account.

## 5. Pitfalls
*Common mistakes and "anti-patterns" to avoid.*

> [!DANGER] Things to Avoid
> - **Mistake 1:** Failing to create copies or backups could result in loss of important documents, causing delays or difficulties during your stay abroad.
> - **Mistake 2:** Storing all digital and physical copies at a single location may increase the risk of loss or damage. Consider keeping some documents with friends or family members for safekeeping
