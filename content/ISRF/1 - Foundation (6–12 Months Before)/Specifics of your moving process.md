# Topic Title

## 1. Overview
*Defines what this task is and when it needs to happen.*

- **Timeline:** 9 months before departure
- **Goal:** Identify the terms, conditions, timelines, as well as required steps of your personal relocation process (e.g. visa).

## 2. Importance 
*Why this matters and the risk of ignoring it.*

- **The Benefit:** You will know the required tasks, their nature and order of execution. You will be able to execute them according to your schedule and personal situation. 
- **The Risk:** Rejections or delays due to missed or wrong steps. Possible double work. 

## 3. Prerequisites 
*What you need to have ready before starting.*

- **Documents:**
	- None
- **Costs:** 
	- None
- **Dependencies:**
	- [[Define Your Goal]]

## 4. Instructions 
*The step-by-step execution plan.*

1. **Step One:** Research the specific relocation requirements for your destination country, focusing on visa application, work permit, or housing lease if necessary. Gather information from embassy websites, immigration offices, student organizations, and other relevant resources.

2. **Step Two:** Create a timeline with deadlines for each required task based on your research findings. Prioritize the tasks according to their importance and the time they will take to complete.

3. **Step Three:** Review the relocation requirements and timeline to ensure that you have considered all necessary steps, such as visa application, document submission, and interviews if applicable


   
> [!TIP] Pro Tip
> Use resources like embassy websites, immigration offices, or student organizations to gather information about your relocation process.

## 5. Pitfalls
*Common mistakes and "anti-patterns" to avoid.*

> [!DANGER] Things to Avoid
> - **Mistake 1:** Ignoring specific relocation requirements relevant to your persona (extra regulations, entry restrictions).
>- **Mistake 2:** Failing to prioritize tasks or setting unrealistic deadlines.
>- **Mistake 3:** Neglecting to double-check the accuracy and completeness of submitted documents and applications.
