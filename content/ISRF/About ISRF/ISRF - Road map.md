# Development Roadmap

## Phase 1: Minimum Viable Product (MVP)
- **Framework Architecture:** Develop and structure the initial framework based on personal needs and direct international student experience.
- **Content Population:** Fill in the core phases with actionable, step-by-step instructions.
- **Internal Validation:** Test the framework against past relocation experiences to ensure all major pain points and research gaps are addressed.

## Phase 2: Iteration & Beta Launch
- **Initial Deployment:** Launch the ISRF as a highly accessible website for easy navigation.
- **User Testing:** Distribute the ISRF to a beta group of current and prospective international students.
- **Feedback Loop:** Crowd-source feedback to identify missing information, usability issues, and varying country-specific edge cases.
- **Refinement:** Update the framework structure and implement content changes based on real user data.

## Phase 3: Scaling & Community Building (Proposed)
- **Open-Source Contributions:** Enable a community-driven model where students from different countries can contribute localized tips and verify resource credibility.
- **Partnerships:** Collaborate with university international offices to distribute the ISRF to incoming students.
- **Feature Expansion:** Introduce interactive checklists, timeline planners, and decision matrices to enhance predictability.

## Milestones
- **Q1:** Draft ISRF content and finalize the initial framework structure.
- **Q2:** Deploy the ISRF website and begin the first wave of beta testing with students.
- **Q3:** Process feedback, publish v2.0 of the ISRF, and establish a feedback contribution pipeline.
- **Q4:** Reach 5+ active contributors and secure the first local institutional distribution partnership.
