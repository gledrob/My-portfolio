# Problem Formulation

## The Pains
When moving from country to country, international students experience significant friction. The core pains involve:
- Navigating the logistics of moving to a foreign country.
- Adapting to living in a new environment.
- Lacking comprehensive, step-by-step instructions.
- Facing continuous research gaps that lead to uncertainty.

## Who Has This Problem?
International students who are relocating for their studies. They face a fragmented and non-transferable relocation knowledge landscape, making it difficult to reuse knowledge across different relocations.

## Why Now? (The Problem)
Currently, international students lack a reliable, reusable guide for their relocations. They need a clear way to anticipate key stages, important decisions, and potential risks during the moving and settlement process. Most importantly, this framework must work no matter which country they move to.

The existing solutions fall short:
- **Existing tools, guides, and services** are typically country-specific, short-lived, and narrowly scoped.
- **Governmental resources** focus strictly on legal compliance rather than comprehensive relocation support.
- **Commercial relocation services** offer only limited or temporary support based on the narrow scope of their service.
- **Open web resources** require continuous, unstructured research without a clear understanding of credibility, necessity, and the proper order of procedures.
