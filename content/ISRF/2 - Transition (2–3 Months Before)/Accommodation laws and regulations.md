# Topic Title

## 1. Overview
*Defines what this task is and when it needs to happen.*

- **Timeline:** 6-8 months before departure
- **Goal:** Determine what rights and obligations does general tenant/homeowner has in the country of destination for your specific type of accommodation. 

## 2. Importance

_Why this matters and the risk of ignoring it._

- **The Benefit:** (What do you gain by doing this right?)
    1. Understanding the legal aspects of your accommodation will help protect your rights as a tenant or homeowner.
    2. Ensuring that both parties meet their obligations will contribute to a positive living experience.
- **The Risk:** (What is the penalty/consequence of failing or delaying? e.g., Fines, deportation, homelessness.)
    1. Violating local accommodation laws may result in fines, eviction, or legal consequences.
    2. Ignoring your obligations as a tenant could lead to difficulties with landlords and neighbors.

## 3. Prerequisites

_What you need to have ready before starting._

- **Documents:**
    - None
- **Costs:**
    - None
- **Dependencies:** [[Temporary and Long-term Accommodation]] (Having a clear understanding of your accommodation arrangements will help determine the relevant laws and regulations.)

## 4. Instructions

_The step-by-step execution plan._

1. **Step One:** Research the specific accommodation laws and regulations for the foreign country, focusing on aspects such as lease agreements, rent increases, maintenance responsibilities, security deposits, and eviction procedures.
2. **Step Two:** Familiarize yourself with any applicable tenant rights and obligations to ensure you are aware of your legal entitlements as a tenant.
3. **Step Three:** Inform yourself about homeowners' rights and obligations if you plan on renting out your property in the foreign country.
   
> [!TIP] Pro Tip
> Consult with legal experts or local housing authorities for guidance on accommodation laws and regulations specific to the foreign country.

## 5. Pitfalls
*Common mistakes and "anti-patterns" to avoid.*

> [!DANGER] Things to Avoid
> - **Mistake 1:** Failing to research applicable accommodation laws may result in unintended violations or misunderstandings.
> - **Mistake 2:** Ignoring your obligations as a tenant could lead to legal troubles and difficulties with landlords.
