# Topic Title

## 1. Overview
*Defines what this task is and when it needs to happen.*

- **Timeline:** 6-8 months before departure.
- **Goal:** Prepare additional supporting documents that could be useful during your stay abroad. (Birth certificates, transcripts, diplomas))

## 2. Importance 
*Why this matters and the risk of ignoring it.*

- - **he Benefit:** (What do you gain by doing this right?)
    1. Having all necessary documents in order will help ensure a smooth transition to life abroad.
    2. Being prepared can save time and potential difficulties during immigration, visa applications, or university enrollment processes.
- **The Risk:** (What is the penalty/consequence of failing or delaying? e.g., Fines, deportation, homelessness.)
    1. Delays in obtaining supporting documents could result in delays with your immigration process, potentially causing you to miss deadlines for university enrollment, scholarships, or employment opportunities.
    2. Lack of necessary documents may prevent you from meeting visa requirements or participating in certain activities abroad.

## 3. Prerequisites 
*What you need to have ready before starting.*

- **Documents:**
	- Health Records (vaccinations, prescriptions, diagnosis's)
	- Proof of Accommodation
	- Proof of Work Experience
	- Letters of Recommendation
	- Birth Certificate (or a copy)
	- Achievement Certificates
	- Extracurricular Activity Certificates
	- Non-degree education proof
- **Costs:** 
	- None
- **Dependencies:** 
	- None

## 4. Instructions 
*The step-by-step execution plan.*

1. **Step One:** Organize and gather all required supporting documents, ensuring they are authentic and up-to-date.
2. **Step Two:** Make copies of each document for safekeeping and to provide as needed during your stay abroad.
3. **Step Three:** Translate any documents that are not in the local language of your destination country.
   
> [!TIP] Pro Tip
> Keep a digital copy of all your supporting documents in a cloud storage service, such as Google Drive or Dropbox, for easy access during your stay abroad.

## 5. Pitfalls
*Common mistakes and "anti-patterns" to avoid.*

> [!DANGER] Things to Avoid
> - **Mistake 1:** Failing to gather all necessary documents could result in delays or denials during the immigration, visa application, or university enrollment process.
> - **Mistake 2:** Neglecting to translate essential documents into the local language of your destination country may cause difficulties with authorities and organizations abroad.
