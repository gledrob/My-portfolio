# Topic Title

## 1. Overview

- **Timeline:** 10-12 months before departure
- **Goal:** Determine how you will financially secure your long-term stay in the foreign country. Calculate the amounts and decide how you will prepare the funds. ==How will you support yourself during your time in another country?==

## 2. Importance 

- **The Benefit:** (What do you gain by doing this right?) Financial security is essential for a successful relocation, as it allows you to focus on other aspects of your move without worrying about running out of funds.
- **The Risk:** (What is the penalty/consequence of failing or delaying? e.g., Fines, deportation, homelessness.) Failing to secure financial stability could lead to complications in the relocation process, such as visa issues, housing problems, and difficulty adjusting to a new environment.

## 3. Prerequisites 
*What you need to have ready before starting.*

- **Documents:**
	- None
- **Costs:** 
	- None
- **Dependencies:** 
	- None

## 4. Instructions 
*The step-by-step execution plan.*

1. **Step One:** Research the cost of living in your destination country, including accommodation, food, transportation, healthcare, and entertainment expenses. Consider any additional costs related to your specific situation (e.g., tuition fees if you're a student).
2. **Step Two:** Determine your sources of income during your stay. This may include savings, scholarships, part-time jobs, or financial support from family or friends. Calculate the total amount that each source will contribute to your budget.
3. **Step Three:** Create a budget plan for your expenses based on the estimated costs and your anticipated sources of income. Allocate funds for essential needs and discretionary spending. Make sure to account for unexpected expenses as well.
4. **Step Four:** Plan how you will prepare the required funds according to your budget plan. This may involve setting up a savings account, transferring money from another bank account, or applying for financial aid.
5. **Step Five:** Regularly review and adjust your budget plan as necessary throughout the relocation process
   
> [!TIP] Pro Tip
> Keep a contingency fund to cover unexpected expenses during your stay in the foreign country. It's always better to have extra funds available than to run out of money due to unforeseen circumstances.

## 5. Pitfalls
*Common mistakes and "anti-patterns" to avoid.*

> [!DANGER] Things to Avoid
> - **Mistake 1:** Underestimating the cost of living in your destination country, which may result in financial difficulties during your stay.
> - **Mistake 2:** Failing to account for unexpected expenses or adjusting your budget plan as necessary throughout the relocation process.
> - **Mistake 3:** Relying too heavily on a single source of income, increasing the risk of financial instability if that income source is disrupted.
