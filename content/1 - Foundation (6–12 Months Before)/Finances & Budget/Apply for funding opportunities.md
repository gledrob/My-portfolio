# Topic Title

## 1. Overview
*Defines what this task is and when it needs to happen.*

- **Timeline:** ASAP (10-12 months before departure)
- **Goal:** Apply for external funding opportunities to get the chance of covering cost of living/tuition without spending your own money.

## 2. Importance 
*Why this matters and the risk of ignoring it.*

- **The Benefit:** (What do you gain by doing this right?)
    1. Reducing the financial burden of relocation
    2. Securing funds for living expenses
    3. Potentially covering tuition fees
- **The Risk:** (What is the penalty/consequence of failing or delaying? e.g., Fines, deportation, homelessness.)
    1. Running out of personal funds during relocation
    2. Being unable to afford living expenses and tuition fees
    3. Missing out on opportunities due to late applications

## 3. Prerequisites 
*What you need to have ready before starting.*

- **Documents:**
	- Valid identification document (e.g. Passport, Residence Permit)
	- Proof of language proficiency (e.g. language certificates)
	- Academic Records
	- Letter of Motivation/Purpose/Intent
	- Curriculum Vitae
	- Standardised Test Scores
	- Proof of Work Experience
	- Letters of Recommendation
	- Achievement Certificates
	- Extracurricular Activity Certificates
	- Non-degree education proof
- **Costs:** 
	- None
- **Dependencies:** 
	- [[Prepare Core Documents]]
	- [[Prepare Supporting Documents]]

## 4. Instructions 
*The step-by-step execution plan.*

1. **Step One:** Research available funding opportunities that match your specific situation (e.g., scholarships, grants, and loans).
2. **Step Two:** Carefully review the eligibility criteria, application process, and deadlines for each funding opportunity.
3. **Step Three:** Gather all required documents, ensuring they are up-to-date and meet the specific requirements for each application.
4. **Step Four:** Complete the applications, providing clear and concise responses to questions, highlighting your strengths and qualifications.
5. **Step Five:** Submit your applications by their respective deadlines, keeping copies of all submissions for future reference.
6. **Step Six:** Follow up with the funding organizations periodically, ensuring that your applications have been received and are being processed.
   
> [!TIP] Pro Tip
> Start researching and applying for funding opportunities as early as possible to increase your chances of success and secure financial support for your relocation

## 5. Pitfalls
*Common mistakes and "anti-patterns" to avoid.*

> [!DANGER] Things to Avoid
> - **Mistake 1:** Waiting too long to apply, missing out on funding opportunities with earlier deadlines
> - **Mistake 2:** Failing to meet the eligibility criteria or providing incomplete applications, resulting in rejection
