# Topic Title

## 1. Overview

- **Timeline:** 6 months before departure
- **Goal:** Apply for any identified relocation procedures as soon as you have the chance.

## 2. Importance 

- **The Benefit:**  You create a time buffer.
- **The Risk:**  Possible delays of relocation process steps and future tasks.

## 3. Prerequisites

_What you need to have ready before starting._

- **Documents:**
    - [ ] [Prepare Supporting Documents](obsidian://open?file=Portfolio%20website%2FISG%20content%2FPrepare%20Supporting%20Documents.md) (e.g., Passport, Birth certificate)
    - [ ] [Prepare Core Documents](obsidian://open?file=Portfolio%20website%2FISG%20content%2FPrepare%20Core%20Documents.md) (e.g., University Letter, Statement of Purpose)
- **Costs:** Estimated fees for the application process (if any).
- **Dependencies:**
    - [Define Your Goal](obsidian://open?file=Portfolio%20website%2FISG%20content%2FDefine%20Your%20Goal.md) and [Specifics of your moving process](obsidian://open?file=Portfolio%20website%2FISG%20content%2FSpecifics%20of%20your%20moving%20process.md) to determine relocation procedures and requirements.

## 4. Instructions 
_The step-by-step execution plan._

1. **Step One:** Define Your Goal based on your relocation strategy, including the destination, desired timeline, and specific procedures.
2. **Step Two:** Research the required documents for each identified relocation procedure, ensuring you have all necessary documentation.
3. **Step Three:** Apply to the relevant authorities as soon as possible, keeping track of application status and any follow-up steps or requirements.
4. **Step Four:** Prepare copies and backups of all submitted documents for future reference.
   
> [!TIP] Pro Tip
> - Prioritize applications with longer processing times to allow ample time for approval.
> - Check if there are any priority application options available to expedite the process.

## 5. Pitfalls
*Common mistakes and "anti-patterns" to avoid.*

> [!DANGER] Things to Avoid
> - **Mistake 1:** Failing to apply as early as possible may result in delays and complications in the relocation process.
> - **Mistake 2:** Neglecting to research specific procedures, requirements, or deadlines can lead to applications being denied or delayed.
> - **Mistake 3:** Ignoring application tracking can cause oversight of important follow-up steps or deadlines.
> - **Mistake 4:** Not preparing copies and backups of all submitted documents may result in lost or misplaced paperwork, causing delays and complications.
